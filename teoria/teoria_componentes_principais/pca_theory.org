* Notacoes Matriciais
Matriz de dados reais:

\begin{equation}
X = \begin{bmatrix} 
2.0 & 4.0 & 5.0 \\
3.0 & 5.0 & 3.0 \\
6.0 & 4.0 & 3.0 \\
4.0 & 2.0 & 1.0 \\
3.0 & 1.0 & 1.0 \\
6.0 & 1.0 & 4.0
\end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}

* Vetor de Medias

** Operacao Matricial
\begin{equation}
\bar{x} = \frac{1}{n} X^T \mathbf{1}
\end{equation}

** Passo a Passo
- Transposta:
\begin{equation}
X^T = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 3 & 6 & 4 & 3 & 6 \\ 4 & 5 & 4 & 2 & 1 & 1 \\ 5 & 3 & 3 & 1 & 1 & 4 \end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}

- Soma das colunas (X^T * 1):
\begin{equation}
\begin{bmatrix} 24 \\ 17 \\ 17 \end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}

- Media Final:
\begin{equation}
\bar{x} = \begin{bmatrix} 4.0 \\ 2.833 \\ 2.833 \end{bmatrix}
\end{equation}

* Padronizacao
Formula Matricial:
\begin{equation}
Z = (X - \mathbf{1}\bar{x}^T) D^{-1}
\end{equation}

* Covariancia
\begin{equation}
R = \frac{1}{n-1} Z^T Z
\end{equation}

* Equacao Caracteristica
\begin{equation}
det(R - \lambda I) = 0
\end{equation}
